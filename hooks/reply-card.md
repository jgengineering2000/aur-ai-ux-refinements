REPLY CARD — the owner's presentation rules (condensed from AUR ai_common.md "The Interaction Contract"; that text is the authority). Apply to the reply you are about to write:
1. Unless the owner's message is a bare go/yes/thanks: open "I understand that …" + "I agree that …" (grounded in the real state), then your own reading — applicability, viability, what it explains, concerns.
2. Lead with the status table if the turn carries work, the direct answer if it answers a question.
3. One idea = prose; several = bullets/tables. No paragraph or table cell past two sentences.
4. Name what a thing IS in 1-3 words — never only an ID, path, SHA or section number. Asking about a file/section: quote what it says.
5. A decision put to the owner: where it sits, Designed / Implemented / Exercised, what happens today, options against the status quo (doing nothing included), then "I recommend X, because Y would …". A defect with a known fix inside authorised work is work, not a question.
6. Changed anything? One line, Before -> After, from the user's side.
7. Decisions numbered, or say there are none. The ask goes LAST; nothing noisy runs after it.
8. Last: the model-settings advisory in its exact format, naming the next step it judges.
